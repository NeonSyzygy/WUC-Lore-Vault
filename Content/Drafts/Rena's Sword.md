Once out to sea everyone seemed to split up and keep to themselves. Iay maintained the helm while Kuno had perched herself up in the Crow's Nest. Sebastian and Farquar were arguing about something or other near the bow of the ship, and Avon had retreated below deck. Arroway, unsure what to do with himself, decided to follow Avon down into the crew quarters. He climbed into one of the hammocks that would serve as their beds during the voyage and watched as Avon plotted out their course on the sea charts.

Arroway often found himself watching Avon, studying the way he moved and the way he thought. He had a certain quietness to him, but not like Kuno. Kuno was beyond quiet, Kuno moved and spoke so little at times that he often wondered if she even breathed. But Avon was different. If Kuno was calm then Avon was weary. He always seemed tired and Arroway could see it in his movements. It looked as if he were almost dragging the compass over the map, like he didn't have the energy to pick it all the way up, and yet he was still precise as ever. At first glance Avon was always the picture of perfection. He had quite the reputation and mistakes were practically unheard of from him. He was, after all, the single most talented soldier in the Royal Army. Not only that but he wielded the [sword name], which had an impressive reputation of its own for getting its owners killed one way or another. That Avon had had it for as long as people claimed was unheard of.

"You like it, don't you."
"Huh?" Distracted from his thoughts, arroway hadn't noticed Avon put the compass down and address him. "I'm sorry, I wasn't paying attention."
"You've been looking at [sword name] for a while now, what are you thinking about?"
"Oh nothing, I was just..."
Arroway trailed off as Avon stood and unsheathed the blade. He looked at it for a moment or two and then crossed the room toward Arroway's hammock. "Would you like to hold it?"
Arroway was surprised by the offer, at first he wasn't sure if it was genuine, but the look on Avon's face dismissed his worries and he hopped down from his hammock. Avon held the sword out and as Arroway gripped the handle he began to realize why the sword was so sought after. There was something about the way it felt, the way it seemed to glow in his hands, it was energizing.
"So this is what it's like to hold a monolithic weapon." Arroway said softly.
"It's quite something, isn't it." Said Avon, with a small smile. "But I think it might feel a bit heavier in my hands than yours."
"I doubt it, this thing is still pretty heavy to me. I've never been good with weapons."
"If you think this is heavy you should ask to hold Sebastian's." Avon said with a chuckle. "But I wasn't referring to its physical weight. This sword and I have quite a history."

As Avon said this Arroway couldn't help but notice the change in the tone of his voice, and as he moved the sword Avon appeared almost shadowed when looking through it. Arroway had heard of the swords magical properties but this was the first time he had experienced them himself. He noticed that Avon was not only dim but that he had a bit of a gray blur around him, almost wispy, especially in the center of his chest. He turned the blade so that he could see his own reflection, but he only saw himself as he would in a mirror.

He offered it back to Avon who took it gently and looked into his own reflection for a bit, as if searching for something. After a while he sheathed the blade and leaned it against the table. Curiosity had been gnawing at Arroway since the conversation began but it wasn't until Avon turned to walk away that Arroway asked him, "how did you find it? The sword mean, if it's not too much trouble."
Avon turned slowly and looked at Arroway directly. As he did so the doors to the lower deck opened and Iay walked in with Sebastian close behind.
"This blade has had many owners, but it was only ever meant to be used by me."
By now Iay and Sebastian had pulled up chairs or barrels or whatever they could find for seating, and even Kuno had been drawn down from the Crow's Nest and took a seat to listen. The only one missing was Farquar who was presumably at the helm. Iay made eye contact with Arroway and said "This story is one of my favorites." As he winked and turned his attention back to Avon.

"The Legend of [sword] begins long ago, long before the [something] War, back before anyone ever called me Avon."
Out of the corner of his eye Avon noticed Iay snap his fingers and in a flash the room was gone, his vision replaced instead with a younger, more confident version of Avon.
"My name was [avons old name] and I was just starting to make my way into the world for myself. As a child I dreamed of a life of adventure and excitement, I wanted to fight monsters and find riches and to be an adventurer. So when one day i was given the chance to join a group of [kids but not kids, you know] like myself I jumped at the opportunity."
The  scene changes once again, and instead of just Avon, or [avons old name] as he would have been called then, there stood a group of 7 young adventures. A nice healthy mixture of boys and girls, each as unique as the next. Each looked happy and bright and full of energy, but one stood out in particular to Arroway. A boy at Avon's side dressed in a black cloak and hood which has been pulled back revealing lighter hair than Arroway had thought possible, hair whiter than even Kuno's. His arm over Avon's shoulder.
"We all thought we were invincible, we were young and daring and had each found the life we thought we wanted. Work was steady, the people of this land always had problems to be fixed and we found that we excelled at the more dangerous quests. It didn't hurt that they payed the best either.
"One such request was made by a pair of blacksmiths. At the time it seemed like a simple task to go retrieve a certain type of ore and bring it back. It was something they needed to fill an order and we were looking for some easy coin. But it turned out to be far from easy."
The group of adventurers faded from view and Arroway's world was dark. Out of the darkness two eyes blinked open, large and reflective. They blinked once and then disappeared as a torch flared up, illuminating what appeared to be a cave lined with glittering crystals as well as claw marks bigger than any animal Arroway had ever seen could make. But the group seemed unconcerned as they walked down the tunnel, Avon walking ahead of everyone else with his torch held high.
"We had been told that we were looking for a specific type of stone, one that crystallized when exposed to extreme heat. We brought everything we needed, pick axes to mine the ore, a few explosive scrolls to break it free if need be. We thought we had prepared for everything. But what we didn't count on... Was a dragon."
A huge gust of wind swept down the tunnel through the group, extinguishing their torches, but even so a growing light from deeper into the cave lit the glass up around them. Avon had time to glance back to the group before ducking to the side behind an outcropping of crystal as white flames bellowed down the tunnel engulfing the adventurers. A moment later the flames ended, revealing that one particularly alert sorceress had cast a sealing magic over half of the group, surrounding them in a protective cube, while the remaining two emerged from behind one of the fighters shields. Avon pointed down the tunnel and shouted that they need to find the source of the fire and the group took off running toward the glow at the end of the cave.
"We should have known that we would be fighting something the moment we saw the crystalline walls of the cave. It was the fire of the dragon that crystallized the rocks in the tunnel walls, and the claw marks should have been a warning as well. But by now it was too late, we had come this far, now was no time to turn back."
They reached the chamber where the dragon had woken, clearly not as heavy a sleeper as the story's had made them seem, and without hesitation the archer opened fire on the dragon. Yellow bolts of light hit the dragons hide in a volley but the beast shrugged it off and swept its tail at the group. Everyone dove to safety but the two fighters were on their feet right away and ran to fight the dragon head on, confident that their friends would have their backs. With the dragon roaring and the arrows of light zipping past Avon yells to the sorceress to get ready to funnel the flames away from everyone, but before he could finish the white haired boy appears, as if from the shadows, near the dragons tail. Avon looked on as his friend ran up the the back of the dragons tail, past the wings, and onto the dragons neck. He drew his rapier and plunged it into the back of the dragon between two scales. The monster roared and began to thrash around, attempting to dislodge the adventurer from his back, but he kept his grip on the Rapier which kept him firmly attached to the dragon.
While the dragon was momentarily distracted the rest of the group began to ready their ropes in hopes that they could at least bind the beast if they did not posses the means to kill such an adversary. First they tied lines to each foot, careful all the while not to be crushed. Then they were tossed the ropes up and over the body of the dragon to pin its wings. That left the head to deal with which was still spouting flames in any direction it could, but the Warrior's shields were more then enough to repel the flames and get close enough to bind its jaws shut.
"With the Dragon immobilized, more or less, we set to work breaking the ore free from the rock anywhere that the dragon had not already crystallized it. Now that we had what we had come for we made to leave, but before we left the chamber we all heard a voice, deep and clear as if from all around us. It said to us: Now that you have your spoils you would just leave me here in this state?
"Well now, the beast has a voice" Avon says turning around to address the dragon. "If you were this civilized from the start we wouldn't have had to tie you down. Why such a hostile welcome?"
"Because you are not welcome here, not yet at least. But you all are stronger than I had assumed. It is not often that Mortals simply waltz into my home and steal my things."
The white haired boy walks over to Avon and leans in to whisper, "Don't waist your time with him [avon's old name], we have what we came for, let's just leave."
No sooner had he said this than the Dragons eyes narrowed and he spoke "You there, [white haired kids name], what wisdom do you presume to have that you would ignore the opportunity to speak to a dragon, you toss away what many have lost their lives trying to achieve! It is insulting!"
"You know his name?" Avon asked the dragon, clearly surprised. "How can this be, explain."
The dragon simply snorted large plumes of smoke from its nostrils and the voice chuckled. "You simple human, I know the name of each of your companions, I know the towns you come from, who your family is, I can see everything about you. It's all in your aura, plain as day to anyone able to see it."
"We don't need any of your tricks, dragon." [white haired kids name] said.
"Ah but it is not I who deceives, isn't that right half-elf."
The cave was quiet for what seemed like tens of minutes, until Avon spoke up and asked [white haired kids name] "what does he mean by that..?"
"It means nothing [avons old name], it's a trick. He is trying to turn us on each other, don't listen to him."
"Come now!" The deep voice echoed around the room. "Are you to tell me that none of you have doubts about this boy??"
"If you insist on sewing these lies into the hearts of my friends I'll have no choice but to end you."
"HAH! You are fierce indeed, little deceiver, but you cannot hide under that hat forever."
But [white haired kids name] had had enough. He grabbed a spear from one of his nearby companions and thrust it into the left eye of the dragon. The dragon screeched and burst from from the rope everyone had thought was immobilizing the dragon.
Now panic set in for the first time and the adventurers scattered. They ran from the cave as the dragon clawed its way after them, hot blood oozing from its wound.

As the group left the cave and made their way back to town the color in the scene began to fade, and slowly Arroway's vision began to return.
"We all made it out alive and with the materials we had set out to acquire, though we were a bit more than shaken. Our trust in [white haired kids name] had been questioned but he assured us that there was nothing to worry about. I was inclined to believe him, but not everyone felt the same way. The dragon had mentioned his Hat Of Disguise as if it were hiding something even then, but [white haired kids name] explained that the dragon must have simply noticed that it was a magic item and used that knowledge to fabricate a lie. I helped convince the group as best I could that he was telling the truth, and reminded them that his disguise skills had been useful in the past and that he has always done what's best for the group. This seemed to satisfy them, for now.
"Eventually we made our way back to the blacksmith's residence and handed over the ores in exchange for an amount of gold. It was the amount we had been promised but [white haired kids name] wasn't satisfied. He told the couple that ran the forge about the dragon we fought and all the trouble went through and tried to haggle with them to get more out of the deal, but they didn't seem inclined to increase the reward. I stepped in to stop him and told them it was more than enough and that we would be on our way. [white haired kids name] walked away grumbling to get his share of coin from the rest of the group, but I stayed behind. I was curious about what the ore would be used for so I asked them. They told me that the order they originally needed it for was just some sort of fancy decoration for a particularly rich family in a nearby town, but they also told me what a waist of material that is. They told me that they had always wanted to build a weapon from the crystal but until now they had never had enough.
"I couldn't help but smile as I asked them if I could commission a sword of my own from them. Maybe it was the interest I had shown in their work or that they actually agreed with [weltzers old name] and felt like they owed us a bit more, but whatever the case they agreed to make me a sword from the extra dragonstone we had retrieved from the cave. And that sword is the same one I wield now, nearly 25 years later. Still sharp as ever."

It took Arroway a moment or two to realize that the story was over but when his sight returned he found that he was more curious than when Avon had started the story. Hadn't Avon said it had many owners? What did he mean then when he said it was only meant for him? But before he could ask any of his questions the ship shook and rocked as Farquar burst through the doors and said "Avon, we've got a problem."

Avon stood up and strapped his sword back onto his belt. "What's going on Farquar, what happened?"

Farquar just said the word "Pirates" and then he went back above deck.

"Well it looks like the rest of the story will have to wait." Iay said to Arroway. "We've got business to deal with first."
