The [[Maintainer|Manifold Maintainer]] is an elected position responsible for directing the enforcement of the law in [[Aeolia]]. They interpret the law to the exact letter of the law, regardless of what the results might be. They are the voice of the law, though they are prohibited from participating in deciding the content or intent of the law.
# Election
There may only be one [[Maintainer]] at a given time, and each [[Maintainer]] must be elected unanimously by the [[Manifold]]. If the [[Manifold]] is ever without a [[Maintainer]] then all governmental functions cease, as there is no longer anyone to execute them. This does not mean that there is no government or that there are no laws, and by extension this does not mean that legal actions cannot be made retroactively against actions taken during that period, but until a new [[Maintainer]] is appointed there can be no government actions other than to elect a new [[Maintainer]]. Electing (or removing) the [[Maintainer]] is one of the *only* powers that the larger [[Manifold]] actually possesses and can execute on its own.
