# [[Maintainer]]
There should be someone at the very top of this government structure, like a prime minister or a president, who is the ultimate authority when it comes to [[Fold]] disputes. This role must be unanimously elected by all [[Manifold]] members, and if there is no one in the role then the entire government system collapses. This way, the [[Fold]]s are *forced* to cooperate and elect someone who will fairly represent all members.

The role of this leader is purely for mediation and punishment of the [[Manifold]] members. They do not make any laws and they follow a strict set of rules for how to govern. They are effectively a formality to ensure no one is cheating, as the role could theoretically be automated or the [[Fold]]s could self police, but they obviously cannot be trusted to do so because no one can. The only thing they have the power to do at their personal discretion is to declare that a situation has arisen where the current laws *do not address the situation,* as in the event of a new technology being developed that the old laws did not consider. They have no power to make laws about the situation or even opine about it, and that process is left to the [[Manifold]] [[Fold]] members themselves somehow.
# Voting
## Absences
Anyone absent from a vote, or who abstains from a vote, is removed from the vote total for the purposes of checking majorities. A vote where 50% of people vote yes and 50% abstain is still “unanimous,” because 50/50 voted cast are affirmative.
## Laws
The required amount of votes needed to implement or modify a law is dynamic depending on the current configuration of the [[Fold|Folds]] and the way they vote. Each [[Fold]] casts their allotment of votes, and then a law passes if the supermajority of all individual votes *other than* the largest [[Fold]] that votes yes also vote yes.

So, for example, out of a total of 100 votes, if the the largest yes voting [[Fold]] is 50 votes, then there need to be at least 26 of the remaining votes also voting yes to add the new law. If each [[Fold]] were individuals then there would only need to be 1 + 50 votes, and if the largest fold were 75 strong you would need 75 + 13 votes.

This means that large super-blocks are actually not optimal for voting because they require you to win by greater and greater margins. Blocks will still form because individuals cannot always be present to vote on every topic they care about, but a single Uber-block cannot simply outvote a small group who oppose them, provided the opposition actually shows up.
